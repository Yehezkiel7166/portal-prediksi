# Brand Protection

Version: 1.0

Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Brand Protection strategy for Portal Prediksi
CMS.

Brand Protection ensures that every managed brand maintains a consistent,
authoritative, and defensible presence across search engines, digital
properties, and external references.

The objective is not only to improve visibility but also to reduce the
risk of brand impersonation, misinformation, SERP displacement, and loss
of authority.

Brand Protection is treated as a core platform capability rather than an
isolated SEO activity.

---

# Objectives

The Brand Protection strategy aims to:

- protect official brand identity;
- strengthen brand authority;
- improve trust signals;
- maximize branded search ownership;
- reduce SERP hijacking;
- detect unauthorized brand usage;
- support long-term organic growth.

---

# Core Principles

Every managed brand should have:

- a unique identity;
- consistent naming;
- authoritative content;
- verified ownership;
- trusted external references;
- structured entity signals;
- stable URL architecture.

---

# Protection Layers

## Identity Protection

Maintain a consistent brand identity across all managed properties.

Includes:

- brand name;
- logo;
- tagline;
- organization details;
- contact information;
- official domains.

---

## Search Protection

Protect branded search results by ensuring that official assets dominate
search engine results.

Goals include:

- official homepage ranking;
- official content visibility;
- knowledge signals;
- trusted backlinks;
- branded snippets.

---

## Content Protection

Publish authoritative and original content that reinforces the brand.

Examples:

- company information;
- editorial content;
- announcements;
- evergreen resources;
- FAQs.

---

## Technical Protection

Maintain technical SEO quality.

Includes:

- canonical URLs;
- sitemap integrity;
- robots management;
- structured metadata;
- schema markup;
- crawl optimization.

---

## Reputation Protection

Monitor signals that may affect brand reputation.

Examples:

- misleading references;
- duplicate content;
- impersonation;
- spam pages;
- inaccurate citations.

---

# Platform Responsibilities

The CMS should eventually support:

- centralized brand configuration;
- reusable brand profiles;
- automatic metadata generation;
- canonical management;
- structured data generation;
- brand-aware publishing;
- asset consistency validation.

---

# Governance Rules

Every brand must have a single canonical identity.

Brand assets should be reusable across the platform.

Changes to brand identity require documentation before implementation.

Brand naming must remain consistent across:

- URLs;
- metadata;
- schema;
- navigation;
- content;
- media assets.
