# SERP Defense

Version: 1.0

Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

This document defines the SERP Defense strategy for Portal Prediksi CMS.

SERP Defense focuses on protecting the platform's visibility in search
engine results by identifying, monitoring, and mitigating threats that
could reduce organic presence, brand authority, or user trust.

The strategy emphasizes proactive defense rather than reactive recovery.

---

# Objectives

The SERP Defense strategy aims to:

- protect branded search visibility;
- defend strategic keywords;
- reduce ranking displacement;
- strengthen authority signals;
- detect competitive threats;
- preserve search consistency;
- support long-term organic stability.

---

# Core Principles

SERP Defense should be:

- proactive;
- measurable;
- continuous;
- brand focused;
- entity aware;
- evidence driven;
- sustainable.

---

# Defense Layers

## Brand Defense

Protect official branded search results.

Objectives include:

- homepage dominance;
- official profile visibility;
- trusted metadata;
- organization recognition.

---

## Keyword Defense

Protect strategic keyword positions.

Includes:

- primary keywords;
- secondary keywords;
- topical clusters;
- evergreen content.

---

## Entity Defense

Maintain strong entity recognition.

Includes:

- organization consistency;
- author consistency;
- competition entities;
- team entities.

---

## Content Defense

Protect authoritative content.

Examples:

- evergreen articles;
- prediction pages;
- editorial content;
- knowledge resources.

---

## Technical Defense

Maintain technical SEO quality.

Includes:

- canonical integrity;
- crawl health;
- sitemap consistency;
- structured data quality;
- redirect integrity.

---

# Threat Categories

Potential threats include:

- ranking displacement;
- duplicate content;
- keyword cannibalization;
- misleading metadata;
- entity ambiguity;
- spam pages;
- unauthorized brand usage;
- broken internal links.

---

# Monitoring Areas

The platform should eventually monitor:

- branded rankings;
- strategic keywords;
- index coverage;
- crawl health;
- metadata consistency;
- schema validation;
- canonical integrity;
- redirect quality.

Implementation details are intentionally excluded.

---

# Platform Responsibilities

The CMS should eventually support:

- SEO validation;
- metadata consistency checks;
- duplicate detection;
- internal linking validation;
- structured data validation;
- reporting dashboards;
- historical comparison.

---

# Governance Rules

Protection strategies should prioritize:

- official assets;
- canonical content;
- semantic consistency;
- entity integrity;
- long-term maintainability.

Reactive optimization should never replace documented strategy.
