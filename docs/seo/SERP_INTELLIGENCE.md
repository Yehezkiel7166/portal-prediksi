# SERP Intelligence

Version: 1.0

Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

This document defines the SERP Intelligence strategy for Portal Prediksi
CMS.

SERP Intelligence establishes a structured framework for observing,
collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting search engine result
data to support long-term SEO strategy.

Rather than focusing solely on keyword rankings, the platform evaluates
the complete search landscape, including competitors, entities, search
features, brand visibility, and content opportunities.

This document defines strategic intelligence rather than implementation.

---

# Objectives

The SERP Intelligence strategy aims to:

- monitor search visibility;
- understand SERP evolution;
- identify ranking opportunities;
- detect competitive movement;
- measure brand presence;
- support strategic SEO decisions;
- improve long-term organic performance.

---

# Core Principles

SERP Intelligence should be:

- continuous;
- evidence based;
- measurable;
- scalable;
- entity aware;
- brand focused;
- historically traceable.

---

# Intelligence Layers

## Brand Intelligence

Observe branded search results.

Includes:

- branded rankings;
- brand consistency;
- official assets;
- organization visibility.

---

## Keyword Intelligence

Analyze keyword performance.

Includes:

- primary keywords;
- secondary keywords;
- long-tail keywords;
- topic clusters.

---

## Entity Intelligence

Measure entity recognition.

Includes:

- organizations;
- authors;
- competitions;
- teams;
- content entities.

---

## Competitor Intelligence

Observe competing domains.

Examples:

- ranking movement;
- content expansion;
- SERP visibility;
- authority trends.

---

## Content Intelligence

Evaluate content performance.

Examples:

- content freshness;
- topical authority;
- search coverage;
- internal relationships.

---

## Technical Intelligence

Observe technical SEO quality.

Examples:

- index status;
- crawl consistency;
- metadata quality;
- schema coverage;
- canonical integrity.

---

# Observation Domains

The platform should eventually observe:

- search rankings;
- featured snippets;
- knowledge panels;
- image results;
- video results;
- local search elements;
- rich results;
- related searches;
- search intent evolution.

Implementation details are intentionally excluded.

---

# Platform Responsibilities

The CMS should eventually support:

- centralized SERP observations;
- historical comparison;
- keyword trend analysis;
- competitor comparison;
- entity visibility tracking;
- reporting dashboards;
- SEO insight generation.

---

# Governance Rules

SEO observations should remain:

- repeatable;
- comparable;
- historically preserved;
- independent from implementation;
- documented before optimization decisions.

Insights should support long-term strategy rather than isolated ranking
changes.
