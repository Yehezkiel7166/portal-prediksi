# PRODUCT VISION

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the long-term product vision of Portal Prediksi CMS.

It establishes the business direction, product objectives, target users, and guiding principles for every future implementation.

This document represents the highest-level product document and must remain aligned with the Master Architecture.

---

# Product Mission

Build a modular, scalable, maintainable, and documentation-driven Content Management System for prediction content that supports multiple brands through a single platform.

The system must remain stable, extensible, and easy to maintain throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Product Vision

Portal Prediksi CMS aims to become a centralized platform capable of managing prediction-related content, editorial publishing, operational workflows, and platform administration through a unified architecture.

The product should support continuous growth without requiring architectural redesign.

---

# Core Objectives

The platform should:

- Centralize business management.
- Standardize operational workflows.
- Minimize duplicated logic.
- Support multiple business domains.
- Improve maintainability.
- Improve scalability.
- Reduce operational complexity.
- Encourage documentation-first development.

---

# Product Principles

Every product decision should follow these principles:

- Repository First
- Documentation Driven
- Domain Driven
- Modular Architecture
- Explicit Ownership
- High Cohesion
- Low Coupling
- Predictable Evolution
- Long-Term Maintainability

---

# Target Users

The platform is designed for:

- Platform Administrators
- Business Owners
- Brand Administrators
- Operators
- Content Editors

Every user type must have clearly defined responsibilities.

---

# Product Scope

Version 1.0 includes:

- Platform Administration
- Market Management
- Prediction Management
- Result Management
- Shio Management
- Promotion Management
- Blog Management
- Live Draw Management
- User Management
- Role Management
- Permission Management

Future expansion must preserve existing architectural boundaries.

---

# Non-Goals

Version 1.0 does not include:

- Experimental modules
- Undocumented features
- Architecture redesign
- Unapproved integrations
- AI-assisted business decisions

Future enhancements require documentation approval before implementation.

---

# Success Criteria

The product is considered successful when:

- Documentation remains synchronized.
- Architecture remains stable.
- Business domains remain independent.
- Operational workflows remain predictable.
- New modules can be added with minimal impact.
- Maintenance remains efficient.

---

# Product Governance

Every product decision must remain consistent with:

- Project Constitution
- Master Architecture
- Platform Layers
- Domain Map
- Module Catalog
- Implementation Strategy

Product decisions must never contradict approved architectural documentation.
