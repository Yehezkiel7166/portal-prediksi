# Master Vision

Status: Canonical
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-07-24

## Product mission

Build a stable, secure, search-focused, multi-brand content and operations platform that can run Brand 1 in production without architectural rework when Brands 2–5 are activated.

## Immediate objective

Brand 1 must reach a verified production-ready state within a maximum 30-day delivery window, starting 2026-07-24 and ending no later than 2026-08-23, provided required access, credentials, assets, decisions, and execution windows are available on time.

The date target never permits bypassing critical security, data-integrity, backup, migration, or release controls.

## Product principles

- Repository is the single source of truth after documented synchronization.
- Decisions are preserved through append-first documentation.
- History is not silently deleted or rewritten.
- Brand isolation is mandatory at every layer.
- Security is designed into the system, not added at release time.
- Automation must be observable, reversible, and safe to retry.
- AI may assist content and operations but cannot bypass human approval for high-impact actions.
- Brand 1 delivery takes priority over speculative expansion.
- Shared platform capability is preferred over duplicated brand-specific logic.
- Production behavior must be testable and operationally recoverable.

## Product users

- Platform Owner
- Platform Administrator
- Brand Administrator
- Operator
- Editor
- Analyst or Viewer

Each user receives only the minimum required permissions and only for assigned brands.

## Core product capabilities

### Brand platform

- Brand identity, domain, theme, assets, menus, configuration, and feature flags.
- Strict brand-scoped data access.
- Brand-aware public routing, rendering, jobs, caching, search, media, and SEO.
- Controlled onboarding through a future Brand Wizard.

### Publishing and content

- Prediction, result, market, shio, promotion, blog, live draw, guide, complaint, jackpot proof, and related public content.
- Editorial state, scheduling, approval, versioning, and auditability.
- Responsive media management.

### SEO

- Canonical URL policy.
- Sitemap, robots, metadata, Open Graph, schema, breadcrumbs, internal links, redirect management, indexability rules, and crawl monitoring.
- Brand entity protection and SERP intelligence.
- Safe automation with duplicate-content and thin-content controls.

### Operations

- Queue, scheduler, cache, logs, audit, health checks, backup, restore, deployment, rollback, dependency review, and incident response.

### Owner capability

- Cross-brand operational overview.
- Brand health, users, permissions, queues, schedules, cache, deployment, security, audit, backup, and platform settings.
- Owner Panel begins only after Brand 1 production readiness and hardening.

### AI-assisted capability

- Drafting, rewriting, metadata suggestions, content briefs, image prompts, quality checks, and operational recommendations.
- Human approval for publication, destructive operations, security changes, domain changes, and production deployment.

## Long-term direction

The platform may later support installer workflows, update management, extension APIs, approved plugins, theme packages, reusable templates, marketplace capability, advanced analytics, AI-assisted architecture review, and distributed operations. These remain controlled roadmap items and must not delay Brand 1.
